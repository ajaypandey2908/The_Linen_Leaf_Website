Plant care guide

Light matters

For many of our indoor favourites, bright indirect light is a comfortable starting point. Harsh afternoon sun can be too intense for delicate foliage.

Water with care

Let the growing medium dry appropriately between watering rather than keeping it constantly wet. Overwatering is one of the easiest ways to stress an indoor plant.

Keep it simple

Clean leaves, suitable light and a little consistency can go a long way toward keeping your green companions looking fresh.
