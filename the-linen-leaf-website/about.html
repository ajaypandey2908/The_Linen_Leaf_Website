About the brand

Welcome to The Linen Leaf by Simran — a premium indoor plant and hydrophonic decor collection where greenery meets understated interiors.

Our approach

Greenery with a gentler rhythm.

The Linen Leaf is built around the simple idea that plants can be more than decoration. They can soften a room, create a refreshing atmosphere and become part of everyday rituals.

The collection brings together indoor plants, hydro bowls and decorative arrangements suited to homes, offices, workstations, bedrooms, cafés and gifting.

Know your greens

The beauty is in the details.

Different plants bring different personalities to a room. Some are chosen for resilient, low-maintenance foliage; others for colour, texture or their ability to make a compact corner feel more alive.

A little care

Let your greens thrive.

Care varies by plant, but the catalogue's guidance consistently favours bright indirect light, watering only when the top layer is dry, avoiding harsh afternoon sun and keeping leaves clean.
